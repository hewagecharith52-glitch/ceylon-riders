Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before you ride

Yes, to legally ride in Sri Lanka you need either an IDP endorsed for motorcycles, or a Sri Lankan temporary riding permit. We can assist you in obtaining the local permit upon arrival for a small fee if you don't have an IDP.
All our vehicles come with comprehensive local insurance covering third-party liability and damage to the rental vehicle. Please note that personal medical/travel insurance is your responsibility and highly recommended.
We do not require a credit card block. We take a small cash deposit (usually $50-$100 depending on the bike model) which is fully refunded upon safe return of the vehicle. You can pay the deposit in USD, EUR, or LKR.
